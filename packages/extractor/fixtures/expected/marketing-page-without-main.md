# Inventory for small warehouses

Harbour tracks stock across shelves, vans and storefronts, and tells you what to reorder before you run out.

## Why teams switch

### Scan anything

Use any phone camera to scan barcodes and QR codes, even offline.

### Reorder on time

Set a minimum for each item and Harbour drafts the purchase order for you.

### Works with your shop

Stock levels sync with the major online shop platforms every five minutes.

> We stopped running out of our best sellers in the first month. Harbour paid for itself by the second.
