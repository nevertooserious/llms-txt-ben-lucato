# Plans and pricing

Every plan includes unlimited clients, recurring invoices and bank feeds. Pay monthly and cancel at any time.

## Starter

For freelancers sending a handful of invoices each month.

- One user
- Five invoices a month
- Email support

## Team

For small businesses that share the books with an accountant.

- Five users
- Unlimited invoices
- Accountant access

## Questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply straight away and downgrades apply at the end of the billing period.
