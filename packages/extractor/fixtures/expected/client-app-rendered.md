# Shared calendars

A shared calendar lets everyone in a group see and edit the same events. Changes appear for every member within a few seconds, and each event shows who last edited it.

## Create a shared calendar

Open the calendar list, choose New calendar, and switch on Sharing. Invite people by email address; they get a link that adds the calendar to their own Pebble account.

- Owners can rename or delete the calendar.
- Editors can add, move and remove events.
- Viewers can only read events.

## Stop sharing

Switching off Sharing removes everyone except the owner. Their copies disappear the next time their app syncs, and the events stay in the owner's calendar.
